## Key Insights

### 1. Market Concentration
United Kingdom generates **85% of total revenue** ($9.03M), indicating strong market presence but also dependency risk.

### 2. Top Product Performance
"DOTCOM POSTAGE" leads with $206,249 (1.9% of revenue), followed by REGENCY CAKESTAND ($174,485) and PAPER CRAFT ($168,470).

### 3. Low Average Order Value
At **$20.12**, there is significant opportunity to increase order value through product bundling and upselling strategies.

### 4. Seasonal Pattern
Sales peak during **November-December** (holiday season), with January-February showing lowest activity.

### 5. Customer Segments
- Champions (Top 20%): Highest spenders
- Loyal (Next 20%): Regular customers
- Regular (Middle 30%): Average spend
- New (Bottom 30%): Upselling opportunity
